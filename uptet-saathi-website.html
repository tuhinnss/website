FAQ

Common questions

Which exams does UPTET Saathi cover?

UPTET (Paper I for Class 1–5 and Paper II for Class 6–8) and Super TET. The app includes previous year papers, model papers, and subject-wise practice for both.

Can I study in Hindi?

Yes. You can switch the app between Hindi and English from the language button on the home screen.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Once you download a paper, you can open and read it offline. Practice and progress also work without a connection.

Is this an official government app?

No. UPTET Saathi is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Government of Uttar Pradesh or any exam-conducting body. For official notices, admit cards and results, always check the official website of the exam authority.

I found a wrong answer or a typo. What should I do?

Please email us at tuhintanmayiit@gmail.com with the paper name and question number. We check every report and fix mistakes in the next update.

Where is my progress stored?

Your practice history and scores are stored on your own phone. Read our Privacy Policy for the details.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026 · Applies to the UPTET Saathi Android app and this website

UPTET Saathi ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. By using UPTET Saathi you agree to this policy.

In short: you do not need to create an account. Your progress stays on your phone. We do not sell your data.

1. Information we collect

  • Study data on your device. Your answers, scores, accuracy, tests completed, study calendar and time spent are saved locally on your phone so the Progress screen can show them. This data is not sent to us.
  • Preferences. Your language choice (Hindi or English) is saved on your device.
  • Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply.
  • Basic technical data. When the app downloads a paper, our file host or Google Play may log standard technical information such as IP address and device type, as any internet service does.

We do not ask for your name, phone number, location, contacts, photos or payment details.

2. How we use information

  • To show your progress and suggest weak topics to revise.
  • To deliver papers and question sets to your device.
  • To reply to your messages and fix reported mistakes.
  • To understand crashes and improve the app, if you have allowed diagnostics in your Android settings.

3. App permissions

  • Internet: to download papers and question updates.
  • Storage / Downloads: to save papers you choose to download so you can read them offline.

You can manage these permissions at any time in your phone's settings.

4. Third-party services

The app is distributed through Google Play, which is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. If we add other services in the future (for example crash reporting or analytics), we will update this policy and name them here.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone.

5. Deleting your data

Because your study data lives on your phone, you can delete all of it by clearing the app's data (Settings → Apps → UPTET Saathi → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the app. To delete any email conversation you had with us, write to us and we will remove it within 30 days.

6. Children's privacy

UPTET Saathi is meant for adults preparing for teacher eligibility exams. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information. Since most data stays on your device, please keep your phone protected with a screen lock.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app grows. We will change the effective date above and, for major changes, tell you inside the app.

9. Contact us

Questions about privacy? Email tuhintanmayiit@gmail.com.
